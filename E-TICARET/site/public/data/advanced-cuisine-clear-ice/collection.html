Stylish Stainless Steel

With good looks and easy-to-clean stainless steel, our clear ice machine will look good in your kitchen or bar while it creates your ice.

One-button Control. Easy to Use

The machine runs the ICE making process automatically. What you need to do is to put the molds in the machine and press the ICE button. It’s that simple.

How it Works

  • Connect the water inlet and water outlet.

  • Place the molds in the chamber.

  • Press the Ice button.

  • Harvest the ice.
