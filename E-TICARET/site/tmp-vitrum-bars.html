The bar that does it all

Our method

Consultation

01
Whether you have a fully developed concept to bring to life or you’re seeking a partner to inspire and advise, we take a hands-on approach from the early stages.

Design

02
We are methodical and collaborative in our design approach, producing visual renders and technical plans that cover every detail from plumbing to finishes.

Production

03
Our technical set-up allows us to efficiently manufacture bespoke, high-quality equipment that works seamlessly and reliably.

Installation

04
Precision, fit and functionality are rigorously checked before products leave our factory. On-site, our teams install and test the system before the first service.

After-sales

05
Training, servicing and long-term maintenance mean our relationship continues long after design and installation, so our clients can run their businesses with confidence.
