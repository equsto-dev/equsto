IMT300 Clear Ice Machine

IMT300 Clear Ice Machine

Mass-produce clear ice in standard shapes

The IMT300 is the first machine of its kind to produce perfectly shaped clear ice in batches—without any cutting required. With the IMT300, bars and restaurants can easily make clear ice in-house.

User Manual
Share
View full details

  • Make clear ice in house
    No need to buy expensive factory ice—you can make clear ice in-house at only 5% of the usual cost.

  • One-button-control process
    Super easy to use—put the molds in the machine, press the Ice button, and the machine automatically starts making clear ice.

  • Stylish stainless steel
    Made with sleek, easy-to-clean stainless steel, the IMT300 adds style to any kitchen or bar while producing premium clear ice.

  • No cutting is needed
    Specially designed molds shape the ice perfectly. Users can simply open the molds to get the ice sphere, ice cube, Collins spear or diamond ice.

  • Mass-produce clear ice in standard shapes
    With the IMT300, you can mass-produce crystal-clear ice in 7 standard shapes per cycle, delivering high output without cutting from large ice blocks.

Produce batches of perfectly shaped clear ice

The IMT300 makes large quantities of crystal-clear ice in standard shapes effortlessly.
